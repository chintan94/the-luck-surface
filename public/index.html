Wait, is any of this real? +

Absolutely not. This is a meme wearing a quant's Patagonia vest. The surface is an illustrative balanced-trait landscape: its reference height is brains = √(doing × yapping), with inputs scaled to 0–1. The colour at each point uses the same fictional score as your energy meter: ∛(doing × yapping × (0.25 + 0.75 × brains)). Doing and distribution both matter; brains improve the mix but cannot replace either. Your dot uses your actual three scores and may sit above or below the reference surface. This is a cartoon, not evidence about people.

Pick Doing + Yapping for Lauris's luck map. Pick Doing + Brains for the boldness-to-brains map: in that view, Doing is explicitly interpreted as the original's Boldness. Pick Yapping + Brains for our bonus remix. All three gives you the completely unnecessary third dimension.

Your closest archetype is the nearest of the ten labelled characters by straight-line distance across your selected traits, each weighted equally. Hidden traits do not affect the match. Exact ties use a fixed order. The highlighted label and your result always use the same match.

Red, amber and green indicate lower, middle and higher model scores. They are not measured probabilities. Diagnoses and percentages are satire. Zero data collection, no accounts, no actual financial advice. Your settings live in the link only when you share.
